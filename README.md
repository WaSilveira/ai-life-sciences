# Applied AI for Life Sciences and Healthcare

## Overview
This repository serves as a **conceptual and applied anchor** for my work at the intersection of
**artificial intelligence, data, and life sciences**.

Rather than a code dump, it provides a structured view of how AI can be applied
responsibly and effectively in health- and science-related contexts, where
data complexity, uncertainty, and real-world impact matter.

The focus is on **systems, evaluation, and decision support**, not on isolated models or short-lived trends.

---

## Scope and Principles
The work organized here is guided by a few core principles:

- **Applied over abstract**: AI as a tool for real problems, not demonstrations.
- **Evaluation matters**: model assessment is as important as model building.
- **Context-aware AI**: health and life sciences require domain understanding.
- **Responsible use**: ethical, interpretative, and risk-aware perspectives are essential.
- **Clarity over hype**: preference for explainable systems and clear documentation.

---

## Repository Structure

### `notebooks/`
Exploratory and applied notebooks related to:
- data analysis
- evaluation strategies
- applied machine learning concepts

Notebooks are used to **reason about data and models**, not to showcase complexity.

### `docs/`
Methodological notes and conceptual documents that describe:
- analytical reasoning
- evaluation choices
- assumptions and limitations in applied AI

## Final Note
This repository represents an ongoing effort to think critically about
**how AI systems are designed, evaluated, and applied** in sensitive domains.

It favors **long-term understanding over short-term optimization** and is intended
as a technical and conceptual reference rather than a finished product.
